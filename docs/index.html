ReVault

A simple, local-first password manager for Android.

ReVault lets you organize and store your website logins and passwords in folders, right on your device. Your credentials are encrypted and never leave your phone unless you explicitly choose to back them up.

What ReVault does

  • Local storage first. Your vault — folders, saved accounts, and encrypted passwords — lives on your device, not on our servers. We don't run a backend that stores your credentials.
  • Optional encrypted Google Drive backup. You can back up an encrypted copy of your vault to your own Google Drive, protected by a backup password only you know. ReVault only requests access to the single backup file it creates — it cannot see or touch any other files in your Drive.
  • PIN / biometric lock. Protect access to the app itself with a PIN or your device's biometrics.
