Privacy Policy for ReVault

Last updated: September 1, 2026

ReVault ("the app", "we", "our") is a password/credential manager. This policy explains what data ReVault accesses, how it is stored, and what — if anything — leaves your device.

1. Data stored locally on your device

ReVault stores your vault (folders, saved accounts, website credentials, and encrypted passwords) locally on your device only. This data is encrypted at rest. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or has access to your vault contents. We cannot see your saved passwords, and we cannot recover them for you if you lose your device or your local data.

2. Google Drive backup (optional)

ReVault offers an optional backup feature. If you choose to use it:

  • You sign in with your own Google Account.
  • ReVault encrypts a copy of your vault data using a backup password that only you know, and uploads the encrypted file (named revault_backup.enc) to your own Google Drive.
  • ReVault requests only the drive.file scope from Google. This is a limited, non-sensitive scope that only lets ReVault see and manage the specific backup file it creates — it cannot see, list, or access any other files already in your Drive.
  • If you restore from backup, ReVault downloads that same file from your Drive, decrypts it locally using your backup password, and writes the data back into your local vault.
  • We (the developers) never receive a copy of this file. It travels directly between your device and your own Google Drive.

Your backup password is never stored or transmitted anywhere, including to us. If you forget it, the backup cannot be decrypted by anyone, including the developers.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't run analytics or crash-tracking SDKs that collect personal data.
  • We don't show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with any third party.
  • We don't access any Google Drive files other than the single backup file ReVault itself creates.

4. Data deletion

You can delete your local vault at any time from within the app. You can delete your Drive backup at any time from your own Google Drive, or by revoking ReVault's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
